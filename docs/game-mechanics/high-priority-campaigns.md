# High Priority Campaign Behavior

Per the wiki's `Second Galactic War Mechanics` page, High Priority Campaigns (HPCs) can have campaign-specific mechanics and often present extreme early resistance/decay signals before progression milestones are reached.

Policy:
- Avoid early hard-fail classification based only on initial decay readings.
- Wait for trend confirmation and threshold progression.
- Treat early values as potentially deceptive until campaign behavior stabilizes.
