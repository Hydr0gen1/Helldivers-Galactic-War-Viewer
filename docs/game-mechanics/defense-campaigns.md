# Defense Campaigns

Per the wiki's `Second Galactic War Mechanics` page, Defense Campaigns are timer-based campaigns with **no Enemy Resistance (decay)**.

Engineering policy:
- Force-null defense decay during normalization.
- Determine defense trajectory from HP remaining vs time remaining.
- Do not treat companion-app defense decay readings as mechanical truth (they are cosmetic).
- Defense operations do not use Operation Modifiers.
